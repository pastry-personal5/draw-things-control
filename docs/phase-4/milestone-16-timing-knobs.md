# Milestone 16: Timing knobs as external files

**Phase:** [Phase 4](README.md)
**Status:** planned
**Depends on:** [Milestone 11](milestone-11-time-estimation.md) landing: its `fit.py`, `forecast.py`, `forecast_publisher.py`, `timing_history.py`, and replay harness, which this milestone reads and then refactors. Also [Milestone 10](milestone-10-stage-timing.md) (done): the `run_stages` and `run_timing` rows, whose runs are what the knobs are learned from.

## Goal

Put the forecast's editable tunables and learned calibration under `data/timing-data-analysis/`, write provisional early estimates and validate later changes against unseen executions, and use factory defaults wherever a value has not been learned. Factory fallback values and hard validation bounds remain in code; operational safety floors are file tunables that the automatic search cannot change. **Tunables** are estimator decisions (the pause factor, how fast old evidence fades, how widely an unseen setting widens the range). **Calibration** is host measurement (step time by backend, pipeline, run knobs, and output width and height; stage durations; trends; spreads). The files are updated by Framecontrol, with the owner's hand edits kept.

## Evidence

**What Milestone 11 plans, and where it falls short for this.**

- Its thresholds are "provisional constants in `fit.py`" and in `features.py`, so a change is a code change and a release, and nothing records what was tried.
- It fits on the retained window of 14 days by default, and pruning deletes the rows with their execution. What one host learned about its own H3 and Draw Things settings is forgotten every two weeks, and the real evidence behind [Milestone 10](milestone-10-stage-timing.md#evidence) is already nearly gone ([Milestone 11's decisions](milestone-11-time-estimation.md#decisions-for-the-owner)).
- The step time grows with size, frames, and the run's knobs, and Milestone 11 fits only log pixels, log frames, a reference count, and the knobs that vary in the retained window. A knob the window never varied is not extrapolated, so the forecast drops to `live_only` the first time a known knob takes another value, although the same host may have timed it a month before.
- The original M11 fallback waited for two iterative-stage readings. M11 now publishes at `JobStarted` from an exact factory point or a broad heuristic when no fit qualifies. M12 must preserve that first forecast and make its source clear while moving its editable numbers into the knob model.

**What a run records, and so what the knobs can depend on** (`timing_workload` in the backends):

| Backend | Workload keys |
|---------|---------------|
| H3 | `pipeline`, `width`, `height`, `pixels`, `frames`, `steps`, `seconds`, `references` (by kind) and `reference_count`, `render_width`, `render_height`, `denoise_reuse`, `dit_layers`, `core_reuse`, `token_reduction`, `ssd_streaming`, `int8_row_fc2`, `reference_rope`, `reference_image_size`, plus `color_corrects` or nondefault `compatibility` where applicable |
| Draw Things | `width`, `height`, `pixels`, `frames`, `steps`, `mode`, `guidance_scale`, `strength`, `shift`, `color_corrects` where applicable, and the model in the environment |

The M10 evidence already shows the dependence this milestone is for: per-step time at 832×448 was about 21 s for 5 s clips, 45 s for 8 s, and 65 s for 10 s, so it grows faster than the clip length, and H3's setup, tail, and Framecontrol finishing change with color correction (1 to 2 s against 38 to 46 s).

[E0053's live Draw Things measurement](../research/time-estimation.md#evidence) adds a narrow, model-specific calibration point: two 384×768, 33-frame, 25-step `i2v` runs averaged 13.33 and 13.95 s per step, with about 8 s of setup, 5 s of child finishing, and 8 s of Framecontrol finishing with color correction. They are one execution, so they cannot establish a size or frame-count slope or the between-execution spread. Its stored workload says 17 frames and 40 steps because the old timing hook missed per-run CLI overrides; the output and child counter say 33 and 25. The fit and replay loader now reconcile this old row. The future ring import must use those reconciled features too.

## Approach

**Files** (all under `data/timing-data-analysis/`, which `.gitignore` already treats as personal data; `ProjectPaths` gains `timing_data`, so no module holds a path of its own):

| File | Holds | Written by |
|------|-------|------------|
| `tuning.yaml` | The tunables, shared by both backends | the owner and the tunables search |
| `draw-things-cli.yaml`, `h3-metal-cli.yaml` | One backend's calibration, with a section per pipeline | the updater and the owner |
| `.machine/ring.jsonl` | Up to 300 complete runs of bounded numeric timing evidence, fixed labels, and opaque model identifiers | the updater |
| `.machine/written.json`, `.machine/validated.json` | The last written digests, values, pins, and update counters; the last held-out-approved calibration and tunables to restore after provisional learning | the updater |
| `.machine/pending.json`, `.machine/updates.log`, `.machine/backups/`, `.machine/lock` | Crash recovery for a multi-file update, its outcome, the newest 20 timestamped copies of each file, and the lock | the updater |

A file carries a `schema` version and is validated before use. A known older schema is migrated under the same backup and recovery rules; a newer schema is read-only to this version of Framecontrol. `tuning.example.yaml`, `draw-things-cli.example.yaml`, and `h3-metal-cli.example.yaml` are factory exports, checked in by the `!data/**/*example*` rule, and a test keeps them equal to the factory code.

**Factory defaults** are code: `jobs/timing/factory.py` builds a `Tunables` and a `Calibration` for each supported backend and pipeline. The research note identifies measured values and labels unmeasured scaling assumptions separately. The M10 evidence supports stage durations, H3 frame scaling over the observed clip lengths, Draw Things step time and within-stage rise, and Framecontrol finishing times. It does not establish a size exponent: the initial pixel exponent is an explicit, broad prior, never presented as measured. The factory records the workload and build of each measured baseline. A missing file or key uses that key's factory value; an invalid individual value is ignored without losing valid siblings. When a whole file is malformed or from a newer schema, use the newest valid compatible backup, then the factory if none exists, and leave the problematic file untouched until `check`, `rollback`, or an explicit repair resolves it. The worker and front ends never raise for a knob-file error.
The Draw Things factory must keep the earlier 40-step point and E0053's 25-step point tied to their model, size, frame count, and color policy; neither point justifies one backend-wide step time.
An E0053-specific factory point has been landed ahead of this milestone in `jobs/timing/factory.py` and is read by the current estimator only when no history tier qualifies. It prices that exact workload and executable build at 13.6710 s per later step, with a 0.99758 whole-stage factor, a 0.205 stage slope, and a broad 0.25 log spread; it does not create or load the planned knob files. This milestone will carry that measured point into its full calibration format and keep its exact-match guard until independent workloads establish scaling coefficients.
An [E0051/E0054 H3 factory point](../research/time-estimation.md#evidence) has also landed for the exact overridden `r2av` workload: 15 requested seconds became 362 effective frames, with 30 steps, three image references, and one executable build. It uses 138.6683 s per later step, a 0.99334 whole-stage factor, the observed denoise and FFmpeg stages, a first-gap ratio of 0.798, a 0.1685 step slope, and the same broad 0.25 log spread. The full calibration model must carry the effective frames and per-run override, not train this point as 360 frames or as a profile default.

**The model** (`jobs/timing/calibrate.py`, pure, `core` and `numpy` only). Per backend and pipeline, the step time of the iterative stage is `exp(b0 + b_pixels log pixels + b_frames log frames + b_aspect log aspect + b_render log render pixels + b_refs log(1 + references) + the effect of each run knob + the size offset)`:

- **Width and height.** A continuous power law over pixels and aspect applies to any size. Each exact `WxH` also gets an offset, the mean residual of its runs shrunk toward zero by `n / (n + k)` (`k` is the tunable `size_offset_shrinkage`, and `n` is execution-weighted evidence so many runs in one job do not impersonate independent measurements). A size used often sharpens and a new size follows the curve. H3's render size enters on its own. Aspect enters only where independent data varies it.
- **Run knobs.** Each work-changing knob has one learned effect (a number as its log slope, a flag or a value as an indicator); seed, timeout, requested seconds when effective frames are known, and reference-pair name are excluded as in M11. A slope is fit only when that knob varies across independent executions after accounting for correlated settings. The file records observed values by backend, pipeline, and model identity. A value never seen and different from the factory baseline widens the range by `unseen_knob_inflation` and puts its name in `unseen` in the forecast's basis.
- **Other stages** (setup, decode, FFmpeg, Framecontrol finishing, including the color-correction one) carry their own log-linear fit on frames and pixels and the residual spread. The file also holds the within-stage trend (kept only where its interval excludes zero, as in M11), the first-gap ratio, the run and execution spreads, and a bounded top 40 of per-signature summaries (count, median stage times, last seen) so tier 1 of M11 survives pruning. Persist only sanitized workload fields and opaque model fingerprints in those summaries.

**Evidence kept.** Raw timing rows still follow `history_retention_days`. The ring is a second, bounded copy of complete, live runs, made from the same reconciled `HistoryRun` that M11 fits (including E0053's corrected frames and steps), never from the stale raw workload. It records execution and run numbers, backend and pipeline labels from fixed enums, numeric workload and build facts, fixed stage and unit labels, durations, and at most 120 numeric marks per run. An unknown stage is represented by the fixed `other` label with its measured duration. A model is an opaque stable fingerprint used only for equality; no model filename, arbitrary string knob, prompt, path, log line, or output name is persisted. A strict recursive key and type whitelist rejects an entry containing anything else. Deduplicate ring and retained-history rows by execution and run before fitting or replay. Imported `log` rows may assist M11's ordinary forecast but do not enter the ring or train calibration terms requiring missing settings. The ring is the gate's replay source after database pruning; if it holds too few independent executions, defer learning. Pruning cannot erase accepted calibration already in the files.

**The update** (`jobs/timing/calibrate.py` for the fit and `services/timing_knobs.py`, `services/knob_updater.py` for the files and the worker side). After each complete run, off the event loop and one update at a time (a run that finishes during one is coalesced into the next). Count complete runs per backend and pipeline, including executions with just one run:

1. Take the lock, recover any interrupted update, load the files, and detect pins (below). If a file is malformed or newer than the supported schema, forecast from its last valid backup but suspend automatic writes to that file.
2. Append the normalized run to the ring, dropping a duplicate `(execution, run)` and the oldest beyond 300. A rejected candidate does not discard the new evidence. Backfill from retained complete live rows once on a new installation, in chronological order through the same checkpoints, using the same normalization and deduplication.
3. At completed-run counts **1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32**, write a **provisional** calibration from the evidence then available, even without a held-out execution. These are explicit early exceptions to the replay gate. With only one execution, adjust only a local measured point or intercept, not a size, frame, aspect, or knob slope; open each term only when independent executions vary it. Clamp changes, validate bounds, and widen the forecast range for provisional values. Keep the last approved calibration (or factory defaults) in `.machine/validated.json` as the baseline, and persist the checkpoint count and last provisional fit in `.machine/written.json`. After the 32-run checkpoint, freeze provisional learning until a held-out comparison can be made; every later run still enters the ring. A one-run job counts as one independent execution.
4. For validated updates, partition evidence chronologically by execution. Reserve the newest three independent executions of the affected backend and pipeline, **all later than the last provisional fit**, as holdout. Never use any of their runs, marks, or retained-history duplicates to fit a candidate, choose its penalty, or set its spread. Require at least three earlier independent executions for training. Otherwise record `insufficient_evidence` and leave the effective knobs alone.
5. Fit a candidate calibration from the older ring and retained-history runs: ridge penalized toward the last validated calibration (or factory defaults) instead of toward zero, so accepted knowledge that aged out of the ring persists through the prior. Cap the prior by `prior_weight_cap`, decay it with `half_life_runs`, and discount it on a changed executable build. Choose the penalty by leave-one-execution-out on the training partition only. Hold pinned coefficients fixed and subtract their terms before fitting. Clamp each coefficient's movement per update (0.1 in the log). Do not fit a term that lacks independent variation or an identifiable baseline.
6. **Gate.** Replay the validated baseline and candidate on the same reserved executions, at M11's checkpoints, with neither version trained on them; apply the same current owner pins to both. Bootstrap paired differences by execution, not by step or run. Promote the candidate to validated only if its median remaining-time error is no worse beyond the uncertainty of that paired comparison, its 80% coverage is no further from 80% than the baseline's beyond that uncertainty, and its values are finite, bounded, and respect justified monotonic constraints. An inconclusive comparison keeps the provisional values labeled as such and retries as later executions arrive; a clear regression restores the validated baseline with the owner's pins overlaid. The log records counts, error and coverage for both versions, uncertainty, and reason. A 10-fold replay over all ring runs is useful for offline diagnosis but cannot approve a candidate trained on those same runs.
7. Write each changed file atomically (temporary file in the same directory, flush, then rename) with a backup and a `pending.json` transaction record that names the prior file set, including `.machine/validated.json` when the approved baseline changes. Update `written.json` last; on restart, complete or roll back an interrupted transaction before comparing digests for hand edits. Keep the newest 20 backups of each file and a transaction manifest for `rollback [N]`. An edit that appears between read and write aborts the update, so it is reconsidered as a possible pin instead of overwritten.

**The tunables search** is attempted in the same background updater after every 20 new complete runs, only with at least 40 ring runs and six independent executions overall, three for training and three reserved for holdout. Because `tuning.yaml` is shared, the search scores all backend and pipeline groups with evidence, rather than optimizing the group that happened to finish the last run. It tries each unpinned searchable tunable one grid step either way, refitting calibration on training evidence for each candidate where the tunable affects fitting. It chooses one candidate using training executions, replays that candidate on later reserved executions, and accepts at most one change per search only when the paired improvement clears its uncertainty, coverage is no worse beyond uncertainty, and no group with enough held-out evidence has a clear regression. The search never uses holdout results to choose among grid choices. Inconclusive searches leave every value alone; the cadence counts new runs since the last attempt, not since the last acceptance. The searchable tunables and their grids are provisional constants in `factory.py`, pinned by tests:

| Tunable | Factory | Searched | Meaning |
|---------|---------|----------|---------|
| `pause_factor` | 3.0 | yes (2 to 5) | A step this many times the median is a pause |
| `size_offset_shrinkage` | 3 | yes (1 to 10) | Runs a size needs before its offset is half trusted |
| `prior_weight_cap` | 12 | yes (4 to 40) | Most pseudo-runs the previous calibration weighs |
| `half_life_runs` | 60 | yes (20 to 200) | How fast old evidence fades |
| `unseen_knob_inflation` | 1.5 | yes (1.1 to 3) | Range widening for a knob value never seen |
| `rho` | 0.5 | yes, while still used (0.2 to 0.8) | Share of the speed factor common to one execution, until spreads are measured |
| `slope_level` | 0.95 | yes (0.9 to 0.99) | Interval a within-stage slope must exclude zero at |
| `factory_spread` | 0.25 | no | The wider run spread used from factory defaults |
| Tier counts (3 runs from 2 executions; 6 from 3), the 80% range, the 2 s publish interval, the 200 ms fit and 5 ms event budgets, the ring size, the backup count | as M11 | no | Safety floors and budgets. A hand edit changes them, the search never does |

**Hand edits** use digest detection, as the owner chose: a file is rewritten whole, and `written.json` keeps the exact file digest and the typed values the updater last wrote. A changed digest triggers a per-key comparison. A changed valid value is **pinned**: the updater keeps it, fits the remaining terms around it, and never searches it. A deleted key removes its pin and returns to automatic. A comment-only or `notes` change does not pin a number. The `evidence` block and `updated_at` are read-only; edits to them are ignored with a warning. Keep the bounded `notes:` block through a machine rewrite, and retain the earlier text in backups. A malformed file is not silently treated as a set of deletions or pins. `show` and `check` expose every pin and the file or backup from which each effective value came.

**The forecast.** Preserve M11's first publication at `JobStarted` when a valid planned workload can be priced. Exact measured factory points keep their narrow match; other supported workloads use a broad factory prior derived from M11's heuristic, with an explicit extrapolation label and a wide range. A sparse current-build history still takes precedence over a scaled older-build anchor as M11 specifies. The basis gains `knobs` (`factory`, `provisional`, `learned`, or `pinned` when a pin touched the figure), `knobs_updated_at`, and unseen knob names. The tier continues to distinguish an exact `factory` point, a history fit, and a broad `heuristic` extrapolation; `knobs` names the source of its values. The live update takes over as steps arrive. If the workload is missing facts needed to price it, preserve `live_only` rather than fabricate a number. The published object, floor rule, and publishing rule are otherwise as in M11.

Rejected: sufficient statistics (`XᵀX`, `Xᵀy` kept per backend: exact, but opaque, a second numeric format to keep in step with the YAML, and the gate still needs a ring); a YAML-only incremental update with no ring (nothing to replay); a per-key `pinned` flag in one file (PyYAML drops the owner's comments on every machine rewrite); a calibration sweep of deliberate runs (GPU hours for knowledge passive learning supplies).

## Scope

In scope:

- The knob model and factory in `jobs/timing/`: `knobs.py` (`Tunables`, `Calibration`, their validation and bounds), `factory.py`, `calibrate.py` (the candidate fit, size offsets, shrinkage, clamping), and `tune.py` (the search). They import only `core` and `numpy`.
- In `services/`: `timing_knobs.py` (load, validation, digests and pins, atomic writes, the lock, backups, the ring) and `knob_updater.py` (the worker-side hook, the gate, coalescing).
- `ProjectPaths.timing_data`, the `fc timing knobs` commands, and `knobs` in the forecast basis on the HTTP, gRPC, and MCP surfaces and in the TUI's and the CLI's forecast text.
- The refactoring of Milestone 11's landed code (planned change 2).
- The synthetic data set's second mode (truth set away from the factory) and the tests below.

Out of scope: queue-wide prediction; recommending knobs for a job; a calibration sweep of deliberate runs; thermal, GPU, or host-load modeling (the ring records the start load, so a later milestone can test it); a second host (one machine only, by owner decision); copying logs or per-run rows beyond the numbers the ring holds; changing generation.

## Evaluation

- **Synthetic data, second mode.** The existing seeded generator (at least 2000 runs, at least 20 signatures, both backends) writes a truth whose step times, stage durations, and size and knob effects are set 30% away from the factory values, and varies what the model does not assume (a rebuilt executable, a heavy-tailed run factor, drift, a pause, a falling step time). An offline report starts from no files, replays runs in time order through the real update schedule, and measures M11's checkpoints. A smaller deterministic fixture in `make check` verifies update, pin, recovery, and replay behavior without running the entire learning curve on every check.
- **Metrics:** M11's (median and 90th-percentile absolute percentage error of remaining time, bias, 80% coverage, time to the first number, share moving back), taken at 0, 10, 25, 50, 75, and 90% of each run, by completed runs of the pipeline (0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 40) and for a size seen 0, 1, and 5 times. Report execution counts and uncertainty intervals; do not treat runs from one execution as independent samples.
- **Baselines:** the factory knobs with no files, and the calibration with the size offsets and knob effects switched off, so each addition is measured.
- **Real data** is the owner's own host, measured once like M11's. Report accuracy and coverage to the owner; neither synthetic nor real accuracy has a CI or completion threshold. The automatic updater still uses its held-out replay gate after the provisional checkpoints.

## Acceptance criteria

Required behavior, tested in CI with deterministic fixtures:

- With no files, each supported backend and pipeline whose workload is priceable gets a forecast at `JobStarted` from an exact factory point or a broad heuristic prior, with the correct tier and `knobs` source. Its error and coverage are reported, not gated.
- At counts 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32, the updater may write bounded provisional calibration, including from single-run executions. A single execution cannot establish slopes across size, frames, aspect, or knobs. Provisional values and their widened range are named in the basis; later validated changes require executions after the last provisional fit.
- A size never seen uses the power law, while a seen size may receive a shrunk offset. An unseen work-changing knob value widens the range and is named in `unseen`; after it has been observed in independent executions, the fitting logic may learn its effect. Report whether size offsets and knob effects improve measured error; no accuracy threshold gates CI or completion.
- A run 10 times slower than predicted moves no coefficient beyond the clamp. A validated candidate with a clear held-out regression or invalid values is rejected; an inconclusive comparison makes no validated change. The ring retains the run, the validated files and digests stay, and `updates.log` explains why. A clear regression after provisional learning restores the validated baseline.
- Writes are atomic (a failure injected between the temporary file and rename leaves the old file), concurrent updates serialize on the lock, transaction recovery never mistakes a machine write for a pin, rollback restores a backup, and only the newest 20 backups per file remain. A corrupt or newer-schema file uses the last valid compatible backup, then factory defaults, without being overwritten automatically; an invalid key uses the factory value for that key while valid siblings remain. A missing directory starts from factory defaults. None of these raises in the worker or a front end.
- A value the test edits by hand is pinned across updates and the search and its terms are fitted around it; deleting the key unpins it; an edit to `evidence` is ignored with a warning.
- The tunables search is attempted only after 20 new runs and only with 40 runs from 6 executions, moves one tunable one grid step, never moves a pinned one or a safety floor, and validates the chosen step on later executions excluded from selection. A tunable's value always stays within its bounds.
- The ring holds at most 300 runs, holds no run twice, and a test asserts every key of an entry is on the whitelist (no prompt, path, or log text). Pruning the database does not remove what the files learned, and the forecast after pruning still uses them.
- After the refactoring step (planned change 2) and before anything else changes, every Milestone 11 test passes with the factory pair and the synthetic replay's published forecasts are identical to those before it.
- Benchmark file load, a 300-run fit and held-out replay, and per-event forecast cost against targets of 20 ms, 3 s of CPU, and 5 ms respectively; report measured values rather than weakening replay to hit a number. The updater runs off the worker's event path and never delays it.
- `tests/test_architecture.py` fails if `tui/`, `cli/`, or `mcp_server/` imports `jobs/timing/knobs.py`, `factory.py`, `calibrate.py`, `tune.py`, or `services/knob_updater.py`; `cli/timing_app.py` may call the public `services/timing_knobs.py` API. No dependency is added, and `make check` passes.

Owner host validation, recorded in `docs/research/time-estimation.md` and not blocking completion: the learning curve on the real runs the host completes after the knobs go live (the first number's error with factory defaults, at provisional counts 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32, and after validated updates), and one real H3 job and one real Draw Things job forecast from the files compared with the actual end.

## Decisions for the owner

- **The knobs are both tunables and calibration** (owner decision, 2026-10-16). Tunables are decisions and calibration is measurement, and both are files the updater writes.
- **The files are the durable memory** (owner decision, 2026-10-16), with a bounded numbers-only ring beside them. This supersedes, for those numbers only, [Milestone 10's](milestone-10-stage-timing.md#decisions-for-the-owner) "timing data follows history retention" and its rejection of a separate store: raw rows, stages, and marks still follow `history_retention_days`, and the ring carries nothing a prompt, path, or log could.
- **One file per backend with the pipelines inside, plus one tuning file** (owner decision, 2026-10-16).
- **Automatic updates with a held-out gate after provisional learning** (owner decision, 2026-10-16): write bounded provisional calibration at 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 completed runs, including single-run jobs; later validated changes require a comparison on independent executions that occurred after the provisional fit. This supersedes this milestone's earlier statement that every write is replay-gated. Every write keeps a backup, and `rollback` restores an earlier transaction.
- **The tunables are searched too** (owner decision, 2026-10-16, against the recommendation to search calibration only). The risk is overfitting a few dozen runs, so the search runs rarely, needs at least 40 runs from 6 executions, moves one tunable one grid step, accepts only a gain over its noise, and never touches a safety floor. If a first measurement shows it chasing noise, the guards tighten through a changelog entry that records the measurement.
- **Hand edits are detected by digest** (owner decision, 2026-10-16). Rejected: layered override files, which keep comments and make pinning explicit, and a per-key flag in one file. The cost is that PyYAML drops comments on a machine rewrite, held down by the `notes` block and the backups.
- **Width and height as a power law plus per-size offsets** (owner decision, 2026-10-16).
- **A forecast at the first moment** (owner decision, 2026-10-16), with a wide range where extrapolated, superseding [Milestone 11's](milestone-11-time-estimation.md#approach) "no number until the iterative stage has 2 readings" where the factory or files can price the run.
